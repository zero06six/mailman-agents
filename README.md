# Mailman Agents

**The AI orchestrator that lives in your browser (and doesn't cost a dime).**

> Tired of paying for API calls? Mailman takes the AI accounts you already have and wires them together—no backend, no fees, just your browser tabs doing the heavy lifting.

---

## Wait, what does this actually do?

Mailman is a browser extension that runs a team of AI agents entirely on your machine. Instead of routing everything through a paid cloud API, it works with whatever AI interfaces you're already logged into (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.).

It automates the annoying back-and-forth: one agent plans, another builds, another checks for bugs—and passes the context between them invisibly. You just get the final result.

Comes with 7 default roles out of the box, but you're not stuck with them. You can create your own custom roles, tweak their instructions, save them, and assign them to specific tasks however you want.

**No hidden costs. No tracking. Just your local browser doing the job.**

---

## Which AI services does it work with?

Mailman sits on top of whatever you're already logged into. Currently supported:

- **ChatGPT**
- **Claude**
- **DeepSeek**
- **Gemini**
- **Grok**
- **Meta AI**
- **Qwen**

No API keys needed—it piggybacks on your active browser sessions. If you're logged in, it'll work.

---

## How it works (the short version)

1. **It hooks into your open tabs** – Mailman open tabs needed for your setup.
2. **It builds a task queue** – You give it a goal, it breaks it down into steps.
3. **It passes prompts between agents** – No copy-pasting manually between tabs. It just does it.
4. **You get the final output** – Consolidated and dropped right into a custom simple interface.

---

## How to install it

Since this runs 100% locally (privacy first), you'll have to load it manually via Developer Mode. Takes 2 minutes:

1. Clone or download this repo to your machine.
2. Open your browser's extensions page (`chrome://extensions/`).
3. Toggle **Developer Mode** on.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `Mailman` folder.
5. Pin it to your toolbar and you're good to go.
