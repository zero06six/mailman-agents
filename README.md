## 📌 About the Project

**Mailman Agents** is a browser-native multi-agent orchestrator built to eliminate expensive API fees. Its ultimate goal is to provide developers with a free, private, zero-infrastructure environment where custom AI personas can collaborate, reason, and execute complex workflows entirely in the background.

Instead of burning money on paid API tokens or configuring heavy cloud infrastructure, Mailman leverages the AI accounts you already own, passing prompts securely and invisibly across your active browser sessions.

---

## ⚙️ How the Extension Works

Mailman operates directly within your browser environment through lightweight extension architecture:

1. **Active Session Hook:** Mailman detects your active, authenticated browser tabs for popular AI interfaces.
2. **The Pipeline Queue:** When you trigger a workflow, the orchestrator breaks down your objective and queues payloads sequentially.
3. **Background Context Passing:** Content is passed invisibly from one agent tab to the next, automating the tedious copy-pasting loop.
4. **Consolidated Output:** The final synthesized result is delivered straight back to your workspace without cluttering your workflow.

---

## 👥 The Multi-Agent Personas

Mailman uses a specialized 7-node pipeline to ensure technical accuracy and bulletproof execution:

* **The Architect:** Breaks down user requests into strict logical blueprints and constraints.
* **The Specialist:** Builds the core technical solution based on the blueprint.
* **The Librarian:** Cross-references the work against real-world constraints and flags outdated patterns.
* **The Red Teamer:** Acts as an adversarial auditor to hunt for edge-case failures and bugs.
* **The Optimizer:** Refines the architecture to make it lightweight, clean, and fast.
* **The Explainer:** Documents the underlying logic clearly for the user.
* **The Synthesizer:** Merges everything into a single, flawless final artifact.

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

Because this extension runs locally to protect your privacy and sessions, you can install it via Developer Mode:

1. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
2. Open your browser and navigate to your extensions page (`chrome://extensions/` or equivalent).
3. Enable **Developer Mode** in the top right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select your local `mailman-agents` folder.
5. Pin the extension and start orchestrating your prompts!
